## Reading / Listening

A cluster of reading lately around what it means to actually own the code you ship with AI assistance. "Don't be a meat proxy" makes the point cleanly: relaying AI output without reading or synthesizing it shifts the cognitive work onto whoever receives it. The "I don't know, Claude wrote this" piece covers similar ground from the engineering side, and Paolo Galeone's "Use Your Brain" argues for CI/CD and ownership discipline as the actual lever, not willpower.

On the more technical end, the Netflix in-house LLM serving writeup is a solid tour of what running the full inference stack yourself actually involves, and the Zod memory footprint post is a nice example of prototype-level optimization that earns its complexity.
