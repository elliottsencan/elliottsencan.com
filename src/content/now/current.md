## Reading / Listening

The reading lately has converged on a single theme: cognitive ownership of the code you ship. "Don't be a meat proxy" made the case cleanly, that relaying AI output without reading and synthesizing it just shifts the cognitive work onto whoever's downstream. The "I don't know, Claude wrote this" piece and Paolo Galeone's engineering standards post are arguing the same thing from different angles. The throughline is that AI as an amplifier requires you to actually understand what it's producing.

On the more technical side, the Netflix in-house LLM serving writeup was worth a close read, particularly their engine selection reasoning and how they handle batched constrained decoding at scale. Good texture for thinking about the serving layer as it matures.
