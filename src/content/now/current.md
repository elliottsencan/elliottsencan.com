## Reading / Listening

The reading lately has clustered around a single uncomfortable question: what does it mean to stay a real engineer when AI is writing most of the code. Paolo Galeone's "Use Your Brain" makes the case for strong code ownership and CI discipline so AI stays an amplifier. "Don't be a meat proxy" is shorter and sharper: relaying raw AI output without reading it shifts the cognitive load onto whoever receives it. The humanlayer piece on lights-off software factories argues the failure is fundamental, not a prompt-engineering problem. Good cluster of pieces to sit with together.

Also read the Netflix in-house LLM serving writeup, which is a useful reference for anyone thinking about inference at scale. The agentic testing piece from endform.dev has a clean framework for deciding where AI agents fit into a Playwright suite. And Marc Brooker's old post on TCP_NODELAY keeps circulating for a reason.
